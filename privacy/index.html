Privacy Policy — Sand Sort Fall

Effective date: October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Sand Sort Fall (the “App”) on iPhone or iPad. The App is developed by Pebblenova Games (“we”, “us”).

Short version: we do not run any servers, we do not create accounts, and we do not collect your personal data ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

The App saves your best score, gem balance, unlocked and selected skins, sound settings, tutorial status, and daily challenge progress in the device’s local storage. If you are signed in to iCloud, this game progress is also synced through your own iCloud account (Apple’s iCloud key-value storage) so it survives reinstalling the App and appears on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple in your iCloud account; we never receive or have access to it. You can turn this off in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show full-screen ads and optional rewarded videos. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow trackingPersonalized ads, measurement
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, regional rules
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and compatibility
Ad interactions (views, clicks, completed videos)Measurement, rewards, fraud prevention
Crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDKReliability of ad delivery

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Your choices about ads

  • App Tracking Transparency. On first launch iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized using it. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in these regions, the App shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice anytime with the Privacy button in the App’s main menu.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time in-app purchase turns off full-screen ads. Optional rewarded videos (for example, to earn gems) remain available only if you choose to watch them.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details, name, or Apple ID. The App only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active, which is used to turn off ads on your devices.

5. Gameplay clips (screen recording)

If you turn on the REC option, iOS keeps the last seconds of gameplay in memory on your device. The microphone is never recorded. A clip is saved only when you tap Save clip, and it goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet (for example, Photos or a social app). Temporary clip files are deleted from the App after sharing. We never receive your clips. Saving to Photos requires your permission, which you can change in iOS Settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads, please contact us and we will help to address it.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google AdMob, as described in section 2.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your data on our own servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings. Contact us if you need help.

9. Security and retention

Game data is kept only on your device until you delete the App. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in the App Store listing.
